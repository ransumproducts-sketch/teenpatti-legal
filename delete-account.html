Delete your Teen Patti Social account

App: Teen Patti Social · Developer: New Creatives

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Teen Patti Social and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't open the app, email ransumproducts@gmail.com from the Google account email you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Teen Patti Social account". We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email, account ID)
  • Your profile: display name, avatar and profile photo
  • Your virtual chip balance, rewards and mission records
  • Your push notification tokens

Game hand records that identify players only by an internal ID are anonymized or deleted. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle, and we may keep limited data where the law or security requires it.

This cannot be undone. Your chips and progress are permanently lost, and no refund applies because chips are virtual with no cash value.

Ad data

Advertising data held by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. Manage it in Android under Settings › Google › Ads.
