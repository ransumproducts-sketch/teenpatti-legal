How we use information

We use the information above to provide the game, sign you in, run multiplayer tables, credit rewards, send turn alerts you have enabled, show ads, keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information.

Who receives information

  • Google provides Sign-In, and Google AdMob provides rewarded and interstitial ads. AdMob may use the Advertising ID and device data to show ads and measure them. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.
  • Google Cloud hosts our servers and database.
  • Expo delivers push notifications, and receives your push token for that purpose.
  • Other players at your table can see your display name, avatar or profile photo, and public game actions such as chips bet and the hand result. They never see your private cards during play.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the service.

Ads and your choices

The app shows optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings under Settings › Google › Ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent to personalized ads and you can change that choice later.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We keep your account and profile data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, profile photo, notification tokens and reward records are deleted within 30 days. Game hand records identify players only by an internal ID and are anonymized or deleted at that time. Backups and legal or security obligations may keep some data for a limited additional period.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. If you cannot open the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page or email us from the address linked to your Google account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Email ransumproducts@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The app is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country. By using the app you understand your information may be processed in those locations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and we will give notice in the app for material changes.
